The First Page

The rain had stopped just before sunrise, leaving the streets bright and quiet.

"We should leave now," Alice said. "The bridge will be crowded later."

"Give me one minute," Bob replied. He folded the map and placed it inside his coat.

They stepped outside together. Water shone on the stones, and somewhere beyond the roofs a train sounded its horn.

"Do you really know the way?" Alice asked.

"Of course I do," Bob said, although he was holding the map upside down.

Alice laughed and took the map from him. The city was beginning to wake around them.

They crossed the square, passed the old clock tower, and followed the river toward the eastern bridge.

"Look," Alice whispered. "There it is."

On the far bank, a small blue door stood open in a wall where neither of them remembered seeing a door before.
